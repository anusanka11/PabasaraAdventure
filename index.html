EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING

Adventure Based Training

Adventure Based Training (ABT) uses structured outdoor experiences and purposeful challenges to create meaningful learning. Participants experience a task, reflect on what happened, connect the experience to a useful concept, and apply the learning to real situations.
